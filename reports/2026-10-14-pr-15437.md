Claude Code stores a session's task list under `tasks/<session-id>/`, but added subscription accounts share session history without sharing `tasks`. Resuming that history on another account therefore loses the task list.

Add `tasks` to Claude's existing shared-directory list. New accounts share it, and switching an older account links it when absent. Existing account-owned directories and symlinks are preserved by the current sharing contract; this does not merge or replace separate task stores.
